Who Am I?

Hello there! I'm Adhenz Miranda, a UAE-based content creator, photographer and videographer, currently completing a part-time MSc in Cybersecurity at Bath Spa University.

I hold a BSc (Hons) in Creative Computing, and these days I create short-form content for Instagram and TikTok for Dubai real estate and property inspection brands, handling everything from planning and scripting to filming, editing and graphic design. My real estate videos have reached 500k+ views for one brand and 350k+ combined for two others.

As someone who thrives at the intersection of technology and design, I'm always exploring new ways to push creative boundaries. I'm open to content creation, photography and videography roles in the UAE, and to cybersecurity roles as I complete my MSc.
